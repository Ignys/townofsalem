Town of Salem is a complex game which allows you to decide how you want to play. After you have played your first few games you can move on to more advanced setups which are more fun for experienced players.

## Advanced Setup

Discuss with everyone what roles and variants you will be playing with. Be sure to check out the suggested custom setups for fun role lists!

## End Game Scenarios

In advanced games there can be multiple factions and determining when the game should end can become more complicated. The moderator needs to be aware of the possible end game scenarios.

Most of the time, the game ends when only one faction remains, however some times a stalemate can occur.

One possible stalemate is a Survivor against a Serial Killer, Werewolf, or any one Mafia member. In this one versus one scenario, the killing role cannot kill the Survivor. The game stops and the killing roles faction wins.

When playing with Executioners or Jesters stalemates can come up as well.

If an Executioner is against a non-killing Town role, the Town wins. The Executioner can also win if the Executioner's target was hanged.

If a Jester is alive against a non-killing Town role, the Town wins and the Jester loses.

If two Jesters are alive as the last two players, they both lose and the game is a draw.

If two Executioners are alive as the last two players, they both lose and the game is a draw unless one or both of them hanged their target.

# Role Descriptions

This section will explain everything about the roles and card layout. Each card in Town of Salem has 3 things: a color coded name, a description of the role's abilities, and a Virtue Value. The role's name is color coded. If a role's name is green that role belongs to the Town faction. If a role's name is red that role belongs to the Mafia faction. Neutral roles have unique colors for each role. To have a balanced game you want to have the sum of all Virtue Values be as close to 0 as possible. A negative Virtue Value means the game will be more difficult for the Town to win and a positive Virtue Value means the game will be easier for the Town to win. The more experienced your playgroup the more difficult you should make it for the Town. Some roles have variants that can change the rules of how that role works. It is up to the moderator to announce variants at the start of the game.

# Town

The Town faction represents the most virtuous members of Salem. In order to win they must rid the town of all of the Mafia members, the Serial Killer, the Werewolf, and the Witch. They may still win if the Executioner or the Jester are still alive. Unlike the Mafia, Town roles do not know which players are on their side. Compared to the other factions, the Town faction's primary method of killing is by lynching the evil-doers during trial.

## Bodyguard (+4)

Each night the Bodyguard will choose a player to guard. If that player is attacked, the Bodyguard and the attacker will attack each other instead. Attacking roles are the Mafia, the Serial Killer, the Veteran, the Vigilante, and the Werewolf.

### Unique Interactions:

If 2 or more attacking roles attack the guarded player, the Bodyguard only saves the player from 1 attack. The moderator will randomly decide which of the attackers the Bodyguard will attack. If the guarded player is attacked and is also healed by the Doctor, the Bodyguard and the attacker will still attack each other. If the guarded player is attacked by the Mafia, the moderator will randomly decide which Mafia member dies. If the Bodyguard guards a player attacked by the Serial Killer, the Bodyguard will be attacked but the Serial Killer will live because the Serial Killer cannot be killed at night. If the Bodyguard guards a player attacked by the Veteran, the Bodyguard will be attacked but the Veteran will live because the Veteran cannot be killed while on alert. The Werewolf attacks 2 targets, but the Bodyguard only saves the guarded target.

### Variants:

The Bodyguard cannot guard the same person twice in a row or the doctor cannot save the Bodyguard from dying while guarding someone.

## Deputy (+4)

If the Sheriff dies the Deputy will become the Sheriff and will open their eyes when the Sheriff is called for the rest of the game. If you are playing the no reveal variant, or the Sheriff was cleaned by the Janitor, the moderator will tap the Deputy on the shoulder when calling the Sheriff the night after the Sheriff dies.

### Variant:

Once the Deputy becomes the Sheriff, he plays with any Sheriff variants chosen.

## Doctor (+4)

Each night the Doctor may choose a player to heal. The healed player cannot be killed that night. The Doctor cannot heal a revealed Mayor.

### Variants:

The Doctor can heal themselves one night per game.

The Doctor cannot heal the Bodyguard when the Bodyguard fights an attacker.

## Investigator (+6)

Each night the Investigator may choose a player to investigate. The moderator will reveal the investigated player's card to the Investigator. Be careful not to reveal to other players who was investigated (through movement or sound). The Investigator has 2 investigations. For 10-14 players the Investigator gets 3 investigations and for 15+ players the Investigator will get 4 investigations.

## Mayor (+8)

The Mayor may reveal themselves as the Mayor during the day. When the Mayor wants to reveal, they may flip over their card and show it to the town. Once revealed the Mayor's vote counts as 2 votes. For 10-14 players the Mayor gets 3 votes and for 15+ players the Mayor gets 4 votes. The Mayor cannot be healed by the Doctor after they have revealed.

## Medium (+3)

The Medium is able to get clues as to who killed a player, and what the dead player's role is. If you have a Janitor in the game or are playing a no role reveal game, the second ability makes the Medium very powerful. When the Medium wakes up at night, they may target a dead player. The Moderator will then point to 2-4 people (depending on game size) indicating one of those players killed the dead player.

## Politician (-2)

You will appear to be evil when checked by the Sheriff, but you are a Town aligned role.

## Sheriff (+7)

Each night the Sheriff may check if a player is good or evil. The moderator will show the Sheriff the Evil side of the Good or Evil card if the player is a Serial Killer or a member of the Mafia (except the Godfather). Otherwise, the moderator will show the Good side of the Good or Evil card.

### Variant:

The Sheriff can find Werewolf as Evil on full moon nights or every night.

## Survivor (+4)

The Survivor must find and kill the evildoers in the town and cannot be killed at night. This card should be added randomly by the moderator to the deck. Players should not know for certain if this role is included.

### Variant:

If a Survivor is lynched all Town roles cannot perform their night abilities the following night.

## Veteran (+3)

Each night the Veteran may choose to go on alert. While on alert the Veteran cannot be killed and will attack anyone who visits them. The Veteran will have 2 alerts. For 10-14 players the Veteran gets 3 alerts and for 15+ players the Veteran will get 4 alerts. If the Veteran is attacked by the Mafia while on alert, the moderator will randomly decide which Mafia is attacked.

## Vigilante (+5)

Each night the Vigilante may choose a player to shoot. The Vigilante has 1 arrow. For 10-14 players the Vigilante gets 2 arrows and for 15+ players the Vigilante will get 3 arrows.

# Mafia

The Mafia faction represents the organized crime of Salem. In order to win they must kill everyone who will not submit to the Mafia. This includes the Serial Killer, the Werewolf, the Witch and all Town roles. They may still win if the Executioner or the Jester are still alive. Unlike the Town, Mafia roles know which players are on their side. All Mafia are called and open their eyes together but cannot kill on the first night. The Blackmailer and Consigliere use their abilities (if they are in the game). Starting on the second night the Mafia vote on who to kill. They vote by pointing at the player they wish to kill. If there is a tie vote no one dies unless there is a Godfather, whose vote is the deciding vote. If only one Mafia member is left alive, they can both choose a player to kill and use any other abilities they may have. You do not need to have a Godfather or Mafioso in order to kill at night.

## Blackmailer (-9)

Each night the Blackmailer opens their eyes when the Mafia are called. The Blackmailer may choose a player to blackmail but cannot choose the same player twice in a row. The moderator should tap the blackmailed player on the shoulder to indicate they are being blackmailed. Being blackmailed means a player cannot speak the following day but may use hand gestures. The Blackmailer chooses a blackmail target before the Mafia vote on who to kill. The Blackmailer votes with the other Mafia on who to kill.

### Variant:

The blackmailed target cannot vote the following day.

## Consigliere (-10)

Each night the Consigliere opens their eyes when the Mafia are called. The Consigliere may choose a player to investigate. The moderator will reveal the investigated player's card to all of the Mafia. Be careful not to reveal to other players who was investigated through movement or sound. The Consigliere has 2 investigations. For 10-14 players the Consigliere gets 3 investigations and for 15+ players the Consigliere will get 4 investigations. The Consigliere chooses a target before the Mafia vote on who to kill. The Consigliere votes with the other Mafia on who to kill.

## Godfather (-8)

Each night the Godfather opens their eyes when the Mafia are called. The Godfather votes with the other Mafia on who to kill. In a tie the Godfather's vote is the deciding vote. The Godfather appears to be Good to the Sheriff.

### Variant:

If the Godfather is the last Mafia member alive, choose 2 targets to kill instead of 1.

## Janitor (-8)

Each night the Janitor opens their eyes when the Mafia are called. The Janitor votes with the other Mafia on who to kill. The Janitor will clean the role of the first 2 Mafia kills. For 10-14 players the Janitor gets 3 cleanings and for 15+ players the Janitor will get 4 cleanings. The moderator should take the card of the cleaned player and not reveal it to anyone.

## Mafioso (-6)

Each night the Mafioso opens their eyes when the Mafia are called. The Mafioso votes with the other Mafia on who to kill.

# Neutral

Neutrals have only one certain allegiance: themselves. Instead of wanting all evildoers killed like the Town, or the Mafia who want to dominate the world, Neutral roles have a diverse set of unique goals for each different role.

## Amnesiac (0)

The Amnesiac wins with any faction until the 3rd night when they remember their role. If the game would end before the Amnesiac would remember their role, they win with whatever faction won the game. The moderator should set aside cards before the game for the Amnesiac to remember from. On the 3rd night, the moderator will call for the Amnesiac to open their eyes and they are given a random role from cards that were set aside.

## Executioner (-4)

On the 1st night the moderator will call the Executioner to open their eyes. They point to another player and the moderator notes that player as their target. The Executioner wins if their target is lynched. If their target is killed at night the Executioner will become a Jester, winning when they are hanged. If the Executioner wins, the game continues as normal.

### Variants:

The Executioners target is a Town role assigned randomly by the moderator. If the Executioner wins the game ends and only they win.

## Jester (-1)

The Jester wins when they are lynched. Upon being lynched they may immediately kill one of their guilty voters. They choose which guilty voter to kill by pointing at a player. Death by the Jester cannot be prevented. The game continues after the Jester is hanged as normal. This card should be added randomly by the moderator to the deck. Players should not know for certain if this role is included.

### Variant:

If the Jester wins, the game ends and only they win.

## Serial Killer (-8)

Each night the Serial Killer chooses a player to kill. The Serial Killer can't be killed at night. The Serial Killer must kill all of the Mafia members, all of the Town, the Werewolf, and the Witch. They may still win if the Executioner or the Jester are still alive. The Serial Killer cannot kill the first night.

## Werewolf (-9)

Every other night the Werewolf transforms during the full moon, and chooses two adjacent players to kill. A player is adjacent to another player as long as there are no living players between them. The first full moon night is night 2. The Werewolf chooses targets by pointing at them.

### Variants:

The Werewolf cannot be killed at night during the full moon.

The Werewolf will be shown as Evil to the Sheriff during the full moon.

## Witch (-5)

Each night the Witch chooses a player to curse. If all living players are cursed, they die and the Witch wins.

### Variant:

If the Witch dies, all players cursed by the Witch die as well.

# Custom Setups

There are countless ways to build a role list to play Town of Salem. Here are a few fun role lists that we recommend!

## Cat And Mouse (5)

This list is very similar to the basic role list but the Mafioso is replaced with a Consigliere who can check for the Sheriff each night.

**3 Townie, 1 Sheriff, 1 Consigliere**

## Blood Moon (5)

Deception and table talk is the name of the game in this role list. With this role list, the only way to die is by being hanged.

**4 Townie, 1 Witch**

## Badge, Gun, Cauldron (6)

The Town must quickly eliminate both evil roles before they are over powered.

**3 Townie, 1 Sheriff, 1 Mafioso, 1 Witch**

## Risky Business (6)

Hanging could become the downfall of the Town. In this role list it is easy to hang the wrong person.

**2 Townie, 1 Politician, 1 Sheriff, 1 Mafioso, 1 Jester**

## Lunar Lunacy (7)

The full moon strikes fear into the hearts of the town. Try to survive against the rampaging Werewolf!

**2 Townie, 1 Spiteful Townie, 1 Doctor, 1 Sheriff, 1 Mafioso, 1 Werewolf**

## Dynamic Duo (8)

The Consigliere and the Janitor are an incredibly powerful combination. If the Consigliere finds a powerful role, they can kill them and use the Janitor to prevent that role from being revealed and falsely claim that role as their own.

**3 Townie, 1 Sheriff, 1 Doctor, 1 Vigilante, 1 Consigliere, 1 Janitor**

## A Murder Most Foul (9)

The excess of killing roles and the presence of the un-killable Serial Killer makes the Town think twice before shooting a target.

**3 Townie, 1 Sheriff, 1 Veteran, 1 Vigilante, 1 Witch, 1 Executioner, 1 Serial Killer**

## More Than It Seems (10)

Secrets are dangerous and can be used as a weapon. Due to the Amnesiac, this role list can turn out wildly different each time you play!

**3 Townie, 1 Sheriff, 1 Peaceful Townie, 1 Mayor, 1 Blackmailer, 1 Godfather, 1 Amnesiac, 1 Jester**

## Little Italy (12)

The Mafia must use their numbers to overpower the town and avoid becoming the Witch's victims in order to best this role list.

**4 Townie, 1 Bodyguard, 1 Politician, 1 Sheriff, 1 Investigator, 1 Consigliere, 1 Godfather, 1 Mafioso, 1 Witch**

## Classic (15)

Inspired by the Classic game mode of the online version of Town of Salem.

**5 Townie, 1 Sheriff, 1 Doctor, 1 Medium, 1 Veteran, 1 Blackmailer, 1 Godfather, 1 Mafioso, 1 Executioner, 1 Jester, 1 Serial Killer**

# Tips & Tricks To Build A Balanced Role List

Don't let us tell you how to play! Feel free to build whatever role list you want. If you want a little help, make sure to take notice of the Virtue Value in the bottom right on each role card. To have a balanced game you want to have the sum of all Virtue Values be as close to 0 as possible. A negative Virtue Value means the game will be more difficult for the Town to win and a positive Virtue Value means the game will be easier for the Town to win. The more experienced your playgroup the more difficult you should make it for the Town.

# Moderator Guide

Being a Moderator can be a hectic and challenging task. However, with a few simple tips you can be the guiding force of the game.

## New Moderators

There are certain roles are hard to track and manage such as the Politician, Witch and Veteran. Rookie mods should stick to the more simple roles.

## General Tips

The Moderator should be encouraging discussion to keep the game flowing at a brisk pace. You can also use a timer to make sure the discussion phase doesn't last too long. Longer discussion phases means it is harder for evil roles. Keep it below 5 minutes of discussion. Make sure to take notes of important things, such as who is cursed by the Witch.

## Managing Players

It is the Moderators responsibility to keep the rules of the game intact. Players should never reveal their card unless told to by the Moderator (unless they are a Mayor). Dead players should remain quiet and not give away any information.

## Shoulder Tapping

There are times that you need to secretly tell a player information as the Moderator, such as when a player is blackmailed. In these situations, the moderator should say, “The person I touch is blackmailed!” then walk the full length of the play area and tap the player on the shoulder who is blackmailed.

## Moderator Mistakes

Mistakes happen. As a Moderator, you will have times where you cause an issue. It might be from not calling a role at night you should have called. In these types of situations, if you can, you should correct your mistake. If you can put the players back to sleep to call the role you missed, do so. If you cannot correct the mistake, such as asking the wrong player to reveal their card, simply do your best to balance out the mistake.

# Variants

Variants are a way to spice up the way you play Town of Salem. You can come up with your own variants or use some that we suggest.

## Role Variants

Role variants change the way a role is played or changes how they interact with other roles. Make sure to read the role descriptions for all the role variants.

## Variable Roles

Some roles are added into the game without the players knowing they were added. The Moderator can take out a Townie to add these roles or get a pile of roles and pick a random one to add to the game without telling the players which role it was. Some of the Variable Roles that replace Townies include the Politician, the Spiteful Townie, and the Peaceful Townie. The Jester can also be used as a Variable Role to give the players doubt about hanging a player.

## Revealing Variants

A veteran playgroup might need to handicap the Town to give the evil roles a better chance to win. One great way to do that is to not reveal players' cards when they are killed. This will make the Janitor's ability useless but will increase the likelihood of an evil faction winning the game.

## Voting Variants

You can choose the way a player accuse one another and how a person is put on Trial.

### Instant Death

On the count of 3, all players point at who they vote to die. The person with the most votes is hanged. In the event of a tie, both players are hanged.

### Process Of Elimination

When voting starts, one player declares another innocent. All players except the innocent player puts one hand up. That player picks another player they deem innocent. When a person is deemed innocent, that person puts their hand down then picks another player they deem innocent until there is only one person left. The last person with their hand up is hanged.

## Daily Variants

If you want, you change the variants you play with at the start of every day phase. This makes it both exciting and challenging for even the most veteran players.
